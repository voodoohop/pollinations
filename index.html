Decision Arena

Grid Rush — a deterministic snake-like collection game that AI models play. Every tick, a decision model on Pollinations picks the next move. Same seed → same 20 pellets → fair race. Reference bots (random / greedy) are free and shown separately, per Opper convention. Deterministic sim → every run is replayable.

How it works

15×15 grid · snake starts center-left, length 3, moving right · 20 pellets from a seeded RNG (mulberry32) · eat a pellet → +1 score, +1 grow · hit a wall or your own body → game over · 200 tick max. Model racers are asked for DIRECTION: up|down|left|right every tick at temperature 0. Model moves are charged to your own Pollen (BYOP); reference bots are free.
